# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect regardless of experience, background, identity or employer. Welcome
questions, offer specific technical feedback, acknowledge uncertainty and give credit for others' work.
Debate ideas without personal attacks. Keep industrial/customer data and other participants' private
information confidential.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation and repeated
disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or project representation elsewhere.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, ask for corrections, temporarily restrict
participation or ban repeat/severe violations. Responses should be proportionate and protect reporters.
If a private maintainer contact is not yet listed, request a confidential channel in an issue without
posting sensitive details. GitHub's platform reporting tools remain available. Maintainers must establish
an accountable private reporting contact before growing community programs; no fictitious team or
response commitment is implied by this policy. A person involved in a report should not review their own case.

This is the repository's own short policy; it does not claim adoption of a specific external covenant.
